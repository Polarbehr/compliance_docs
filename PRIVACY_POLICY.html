Privacy Policy for Nexus Secure Chat

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Nexus Secure Chat is a Zero-Trust application. We do not collect, store, or have access to your messages, cryptographic keys, or identity data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Nexus Secure Chat does not require account registration. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Camera Permission (Required)

The application requires access to your device's Camera. This permission is used exclusively for scanning PGP Public Key QR codes from other users to establish encrypted communication channels. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Data Storage and Encryption

All message data and cryptographic keys are stored locally on your device in an encrypted vault. We utilize industry-standard PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) for end-to-end encryption. Since we do not hold your private keys, we cannot decrypt or recover your data under any circumstances.

4. Data Deletion (E.L.E. Protocol)

The application includes an "Extinction Level Event" (E.L.E.) protocol that allows users to instantly and permanently wipe all local and server-side relay data. This action is irreversible.

5. Third-Party Services

The application utilizes Google Play Integrity API to ensure the security and integrity of the application environment. This service may collect certain device telemetry as governed by Google's privacy policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our secure relay support channels.
